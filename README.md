# Freelancing Career Mastery

Live website: https://sonjibonbarua.github.io/Freelancing-Career-Mastery/

A complete Basic-to-Advanced freelancing learning platform built as 8 career modules and 64 live lessons.

## Curriculum

1. Freelancing Foundation — Lessons 1–6
2. Skill, Niche & Portfolio — Lessons 7–13
3. Online Presence & Personal Brand — Lessons 14–20
4. Finding Clients — Lessons 21–29
5. Client Communication & Sales — Lessons 30–38
6. Project Management & Delivery — Lessons 39–46
7. Money, Risk & Freelance Business — Lessons 47–53
8. Advanced Career Growth — Lessons 54–64

## Lesson media system

Every lesson has a topic-specific visual model, for example: value exchange, mindset ladders, decision trees, portfolio trust stacks, acquisition funnels, proposal architecture, scope-control flows, payment protection, risk radar, retention flywheels, AI workflow, delegation and long-term career strategy.

Every lesson also has a companion video selected to reinforce the specific topic. Strong resources may be reused across tightly related lessons, but each placement includes lesson-specific context and three watch-for prompts so the video supports the current learning objective instead of acting like a generic playlist.

## Real-Life Freelancer Survival Lab

The dedicated Survival Lab is an interactive scenario simulator covering:

- Client ghosting
- Late payment
- Scope creep
- Endless revisions
- Difficult clients and availability pressure
- Fake clients and scams
- Project cancellation
- Refund disputes
- Missed deadlines
- Bad feedback
- Client retention
- Confidentiality
- Professional boundaries
- Burnout and sustainable workload

Readers choose how they would react under pressure, receive immediate feedback, then see professional reasoning, a sample response, red flags, prevention systems and next actions.

The upgraded lab also includes:

- A Judgment Profile showing Strong / Mixed / High-risk decision patterns
- A personal reflection field for every case
- Saved decision and reflection history on the learner’s device
- A final debrief that interprets decision patterns without treating the simulator as a simplistic pass/fail test

## Product standard

The course is designed around four stages: **Learn → Apply → Reflect → Pressure-test**. Each lesson should leave the learner with a clearer mental model, a concrete output or action, evidence of understanding, and a personal decision rule they can carry into real client work.

## Notes

Platform interfaces, marketplace policies, legal requirements and tax rules can change. Lessons focus on durable professional principles and remind learners to verify current platform or local-jurisdiction requirements when relevant.
