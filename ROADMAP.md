# Freelancing Career Mastery — Curriculum Roadmap

This repository is being developed as a structured Basic-to-Advanced freelancing learning platform.

## Module 1 — Freelancing Foundation (Lessons 1–6)
1. Freelancing Explained: Career, Business & Value
2. Freelancer Mindset: From Earner to Problem Solver
3. Skills, Services & Client Value
4. Understanding Client Problems: Known vs Unknown Phase — **Published**
5. Choosing Problems Worth Solving
6. Building Your Personal Freelancing Roadmap

## Module 2 — Skill, Niche & Portfolio (Lessons 7–13)
Covers strategic skill selection, deliberate practice, turning skills into services, niche selection, portfolio strategy, case studies, and positioning.

## Module 3 — Online Presence & Personal Brand (Lessons 14–20)
Covers professional profiles, personal branding, platform selection, freelancer content strategy, consistency, algorithms/niche signals, and trust building.

## Module 4 — Finding Clients (Lessons 21–29)
Covers ideal clients, marketplaces, LinkedIn, content-led acquisition, prospect research, cold outreach, referrals, communities, and repeatable lead generation.

## Module 5 — Client Communication & Sales (Lessons 30–38)
Covers first messages, discovery, better questions, requirements, proposals, pricing, negotiation, objections, and closing clients.

## Module 6 — Project Management & Delivery (Lessons 39–46)
Covers onboarding, scope, milestones, deadlines, feedback, revisions, scope creep, quality control, handoff, testimonials, and long-term relationships.

## Module 7 — Money, Risk & Freelance Business (Lessons 47–53)
Covers pricing models, invoices, deposits, payment safety, contracts, professional boundaries, income/expense tracking, irregular income, scams, and risk management.

## Module 8 — Advanced Career Growth (Lessons 54–64)
Covers retention, retainers, recurring revenue, upselling, high-ticket specialization, authority, systems, AI-assisted workflows, outsourcing, agency transition, burnout prevention, and long-term career design.

## Real-Life Survival Topics
The course will also include practical case-based coverage for client ghosting, late payment, scope creep, endless revisions, difficult clients, fake clients/scams, project cancellation, refunds/disputes, missed deadlines, bad feedback, client retention, confidentiality, professional boundaries, and burnout.

## Publishing Rule
A lesson appears as **Available** only after its full lesson page and learning activities are ready. Curriculum placeholders remain marked **Planned** until then.
