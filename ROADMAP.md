# Freelancing Career Mastery — Curriculum Roadmap

This repository now contains a complete Basic-to-Advanced freelancing learning path with 8 modules, 64 live lessons, interactive practice, completion tracking, selected external learning videos, and a dedicated Real-Life Freelancer Survival Lab.

## Module 1 — Freelancing Foundation (Lessons 1–6) — **Published**
Freelancing fundamentals, problem-solving mindset, skills/services/value, client problems, problem selection, and personal career roadmap.

## Module 2 — Skill, Niche & Portfolio (Lessons 7–13) — **Published**
Strategic skill selection, deliberate practice, service design, niche selection, portfolio strategy, case studies, and positioning.

## Module 3 — Online Presence & Personal Brand (Lessons 14–20) — **Published**
Professional profiles, personal branding, platform selection, content strategy, consistency, algorithms/niche signals, and trust building.

## Module 4 — Finding Clients (Lessons 21–29) — **Published**
Ideal clients, acquisition channels, marketplaces, LinkedIn/networking, organic acquisition, prospect research, cold outreach, referrals, and repeatable lead generation.

## Module 5 — Client Communication & Sales (Lessons 30–38) — **Published**
First messages, discovery, better questions, requirements/scope, proposals, pricing, negotiation, objections, and closing healthy client agreements.

## Module 6 — Project Management & Delivery (Lessons 39–46) — **Published**
Onboarding, project planning, milestones, deadlines, communication, revisions/scope creep, QA, handoff, proof capture, and long-term client relationships.

## Module 7 — Money, Risk & Freelance Business (Lessons 47–53) — **Published**
Pricing models, invoices, deposits, payment safety, contracts/boundaries, financial tracking, irregular income, scams, and risk management. Legal/tax material is educational and should be verified for the learner's local jurisdiction.

## Module 8 — Advanced Career Growth (Lessons 54–64) — **Published**
Retention, retainers, value expansion, high-value specialization, authority, systems, AI-assisted workflows, outsourcing, agency transition, burnout prevention, and long-term career design.

## Real-Life Freelancer Survival Lab — **Published**
Interactive case-based practice covering client ghosting, late payment, scope creep, endless revisions, difficult clients, fake clients/scams, cancellation, refund disputes, missed deadlines, bad feedback, client retention, confidentiality, professional boundaries, and burnout.

Each Survival Lab case includes the client message, the emotional pressure, multiple possible decisions, immediate feedback, professional reasoning, a sample response, red flags, prevention systems, and next actions.

## External Learning Videos
Selected lessons include companion video resources from credible public educators/channels. These resources remain external; platform interfaces and policies can change, so learners are reminded to verify current rules when necessary.
