📘 CLASS 11 • FREELANCING FOUNDATION

Understanding the
Foundation of Freelancing

Freelancing is not only about earning money. It is about understanding a client's problem, creating value, and providing the right solution.

Start lesson ↓ ◷ About 4 min read
01 • CORE CONCEPT

Two phases of client problems

A client's situation can generally be understood in two ways: a problem they already recognize, or a problem they have not yet identified.

✓
KNOWN PHASE

The client knows the problem.

The client already understands what they need and is actively looking for someone who can provide the solution.

01 Problem identified → Solution needed
?
UNKNOWN PHASE

The client has not identified the problem.

The freelancer first helps the client recognize the issue, then explains and offers an appropriate solution.

02 Problem discovered → Solution offered
02
TODAY'S CLASS • KNOWN PHASE

Build a presence where clients are already searching.

In the Known Phase, clients already know what they need and often search for solutions on social media. Since our current skill is graphic design, our social media presence should clearly communicate that we are graphic designers.

The goal is simple: consistently share design work, graphic-design posts, and relevant videos. Over time, this helps the platform understand our niche and can increase the chance of our work reaching the right clients.

IMPORTANT MINDSET

Do not measure the beginning by likes and views.

The first few days can feel discouraging when posts receive little attention. Instead of losing motivation, focus on building a professional-looking profile and posting regularly. At the beginning, consistency matters more than immediate engagement.

03 • KEY TAKEAWAYS

What to remember

Four simple principles from this lesson to carry into your freelancing journey.

01

Solve problems

Focus on creating value for clients, not only on earning money.

02

Build your presence

Make your social profile clearly represent you as a graphic designer.

03

Stay consistent

Keep sharing relevant work so platforms can better understand your niche.

04

Ignore early numbers

Do not let low likes or views stop you from continuing to publish.
